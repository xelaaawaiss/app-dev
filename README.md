# My Favorite Movie: *Superman*
---
## Main Characters

1. **Clark Kent / Superman** – The hero from Krypton with superhuman strength and flight.  
2. **Lois Lane** – A fearless journalist and Superman’s love interest.  
3. **Lex Luthor** – A genius villain obsessed with defeating Superman.  
4. **Martha and Jonathan Kent** – Clark’s adoptive parents who teach him humility and kindness.
